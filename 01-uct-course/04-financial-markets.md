## Key questions this module answers
- How have financial markets evolved over time?
- Where does blockchain fit into that evolution?
- What changes when markets become more digital and programmable?

---

## Main concepts to cover

### 1. Traditional financial market structure
- Key players (exchanges, brokers, custodians, clearing houses)
- How value and information currently move

### 2. Digitisation of markets
- Electronic trading
- Existing infrastructure limitations

### 3. Blockchain’s potential impact
- Faster settlement
- Reduced intermediaries
- 24/7 markets
- Atomic settlement / delivery-versus-payment

### 4. Emerging market perspective
- Opportunities and constraints in places like South Africa
- Access, inclusion, and infrastructure gaps
